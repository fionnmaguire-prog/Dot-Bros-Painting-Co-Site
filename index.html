Our Story.

Dot Bros Painting Co. was founded by two artists and filmmakers who came to Los Angeles to build careers in the creative industries.

Outside of painting homes, you'll usually find us making films, creating artwork, DJing, producing music, designing murals and working on the many creative projects that inspire us. Painting allows us to build a business we're proud of while continuing to pursue the creative work we're passionate about.

We believe every project deserves the same creativity, attention to detail and pride that we bring to our own artistic work. Whether we're repainting a bedroom or creating a custom mural, our goal is simple: deliver quality workmanship, treat people with respect and leave every space looking better than we found it.

When you choose Dot Bros, you're supporting two local creatives building a future in Los Angeles while delivering a professional painting service you can rely on.
